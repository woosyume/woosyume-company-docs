Jikyuchan

Smart wishlist app

Would you spend three hours at work for something that costs ¥4,500? Jikyuchan uses your own hourly wage to help you decide, with a little company along the way.

View on the App StoreProduct website

Inside the app

See a price in hours

Enter your hourly wage and the price of something you’re considering. Jikyuchan shows how much working time it would take.

Give a decision some time

Save a product you’re eyeing and come back to it later. Record what you decided when you’re ready.

Look back with Jikyuchan

Revisit the things you bought or passed on, with Jikyuchan and friends alongside you. The app is in Japanese.
