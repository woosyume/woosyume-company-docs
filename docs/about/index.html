About the developer

Hi, I’m Aaron.

I’m Aaron Kim, a technical instructor and software developer based in Osaka, Japan. THE WOOSYUME COMPANY is where I publish the apps I make for Mac and iPhone.

When I give a presentation, teach a class, or run a demo, I notice every extra click that interrupts an explanation. Those moments shape the software I build. I want the tools I use to give me more time for the people I’m working with.
